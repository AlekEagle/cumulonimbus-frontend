01 / THE FLAW

Why a blank page becomes a shell

Notepad2Shell is a design-level vulnerability in the text editor. When Notepad launches, it offers a fully interactive input surface and writes every keystroke into a buffer with the privileges of the person sitting there. Notepad does not run the commands. It writes them down. That is the vulnerability, and also the product. Saving the buffer as shell.bat is when the metaphor ends and the double-click begins.

The attack is a walk down the menu bar. No radio, no pairing, no second device. Just File, Edit, Format, and a filename you should not have chosen.

04 / DEFENSE

Protect your documents

No vendor patch is available. Until one is released, these help a little.

01

Do not open Notepad

The most effective control. Opening it “to paste a command you are afraid to run” is the command.

02

Do not press Save

The asterisk is a promise you can break. The document is still a session. It is just a shy one.

03

Keep the extension .txt

Renaming the file is the exploit, the patch, and the file’s entire career. Cowardice is a control.

04

Do not press F5

It inserts the date, and the date is not an answer. It is also the only command Notepad ever shipped.

05

Enable Word Wrap

A command that does not fit on one line cannot finish its sentence. This is not how shells work. It feels responsible, which is why the Format menu offers it.

06

Close without saving

If the dialog asks whether you want to save changes, that question is the ransom. Choose No, then walk away from the idea that this was meeting notes.
